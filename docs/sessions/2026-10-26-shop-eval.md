# Stage 3: the Shop Mode eval

Date: 2026-10-26. Mock mode and fixtures only; no live calls, no live eval.

## What changed and why
A harness, in mock mode, to measure Shop Mode: 12 requests, each with a hidden persona, scored on first-batch quality and on
whether Shop Again moves results toward the persona. All of it lives under `evals/shop/` (see its README).
- **Cases and thresholds** (phase 1): the owner's 12 requests unchanged (`cases/case-NN.json`, index, changelog), personas that do
  not restate the request, `thresholds.json` with `THRESHOLDS.md` (judgment values, hash recorded in every result).
- **Judges and scorers** (phase 2): code scorers for price, repeats, sellers, near-duplicates (the pipeline's `nearby.py`),
  context leakage, colour shares, refine exclusion and focus, rates; threshold evaluation; two Haiku judges (category and
  attribute, persona) on prompts `eval_shop_category_judge` and `eval_shop_persona_judge` v1: forced tool call, strict schema,
  thinking off, 12 items per call, thumbnails through the guarded fetch, validated, one retry, then "judge failed".
- **Runner and targets** (phase 3): search, then Shop Again twice through the service layer; the persona rates the first 12 in
  display order; refine steps; hard stops ($0.60 Anthropic, 500 credits) with partial results; a failing case is recorded and
  the run continues. `make eval-shop-plan`, `eval-shop-mock`, `eval-shop-live` (needs `EVAL_SHOP_CONFIRM=yes`, refuses under CI
  and pytest), `eval-shop-report`.
- **Reports and docs** (phase 4): Markdown report without thumbnails or links, known-instability file, README, ADR 0012 stage 3
  amendment, SPEC, todo, working notes, and the fifth replay fixture.

## Repo conventions
The repo's evals use `evals/cases/*.jsonl`, `thresholds.yaml`, gitignored `evals/reports/*.json`, `known-unstable/` and mock-forced
Makefile targets. The shop eval follows that for mock/live handling, results and the known-unstable convention, and the brief for
file shapes (one JSON per case, `thresholds.json`). It is not wired into `make eval-fast` (that runs the template's placeholder
pipeline). `pyproject.toml` gained pytest `pythonpath = ["."]` so tests can import `evals`.

## Category findings (kept as written, no substitution)
curtains and bookshelves are not in `categories.py`'s synonym groups or size table; "dining chairs" and "dining chair" are
different topic keys; "desk chairs" folds into "desk". Also in mock: "focus on mesh backs" reads as an approximate free note (no
`mesh` in the vocabulary), so the focus share fails in mock.

## Estimate from `make eval-shop-plan` (an estimate, not a measurement)
Anthropic about $0.43 (pipeline $0.36, judges $0.07 over 144 calls), Serper 240 credits expected (480 worst case, about $0.24),
total about $0.67. Inside the $0.60 Anthropic stop and the 500-credit stop, but only just on dollars: the pipeline cost per case is
about $0.050 against the $0.05 threshold (judges not included). Assumptions are printed by the plan.

## Mock run (`make eval-shop-mock`)
All 12 cases complete, 36 batches, 0 repeats, no judge failures, about $0.04 and 196 credits (mock numbers). The verdict is
"fail" on the quality thresholds (attribute match, lift, refine focus): expected, and not meaningful, because the fixtures and the
stand-in judges are keyword rules. The mock target exits 0 when every case completes.
